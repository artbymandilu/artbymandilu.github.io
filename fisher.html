Fisher House: A Precedent Study

A study exploring Louis Kahn's Fisher House as a testing ground of spatial orders and environmental control in a domestic space.

Academic Term: 2026 Spring

Instructor: Tonya Markiewicz

Project Description

Louis Kahn's Fisher House was designed in 1967 for Mrs. Doris Fisher and Dr. Norman Fisher, a practicing physician. Living in Hatboro, Pennsylvania, the family sought both seclusion in the woods and space for Dr. Fisher to receive his patients. This spatial conversation between public and privacy makes the house an ideal site for exploring architectural hierarchy.
