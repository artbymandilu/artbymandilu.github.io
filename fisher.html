Fisher House: A Precedence Study

A study exploring the relationship between precedent, spatial organization, and architectural language.

Project Description

This project examined the Fisher House as an architectural precedent, analyzing its spatial qualities, material relationships, and design strategies. Through drawings and physical modeling, the study explored how architectural decisions shape movement, experience, and connection to place.
