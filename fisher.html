Fisher House: A Precedent Study

A study exploring Louis Kahn's Fisher House as a testing ground of spacial orders and enviormental control in a domestic space

Project Description

Louis Kahn's Fisher House was designed in 1967 for Mrs. Doris Fisher and Dr. Norman Fisher, a practicing physician. Living in Hatboro Pennsylvania, the family sought both seclusion in the woods and space for Dr. Fisher to recieve his patients. This spatial conversation between public and privacy makes the house an ideal site for exploring architectual hierarchy.
